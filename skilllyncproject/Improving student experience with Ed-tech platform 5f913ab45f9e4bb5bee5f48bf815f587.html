Improving student experience with Ed-tech platform

Skill-Lync is an ed-tech platform that provides industry-relevant technical courses in the domains of Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, Electronics, Medical Technology & Computer Science Engineering.

The company has diversified by providing jobs with job assistance programs. The company also does B2B business with educational institutions and companies. (Corporate-upskilling)

I was with the product team. There were four to five PMs and two designers. The team was split into pre-sales and post-sales teams. Our major project was to revamp the entire product experience.

Pre-sales

The pre-sales process is responsible for reaching out to potential students and companies to promote Skill-Lync's courses and services. This includes creating marketing materials, attending events, and communicating with potential clients to understand their needs and interests. This also provides guidance and support to students and companies during the onboarding process.


This process includes course selection, demo call, TSE speaking with the student, sales call, payment, loan processing, onboarding, orientation program, TD call, and POC call, and continues until the student is fully onboarded to the LMS.

Post-sales

The post-sales process is responsible for ensuring that students and companies have a positive experience with Skill-Lync's courses and services. This includes providing support for technical issues, answering questions about course material, and addressing any concerns that students or companies may have. The team also works on improving the overall user experience of the platform by incorporating feedback from users and making necessary changes to the platform.

In addition, the post-sales team is responsible for ensuring that students and companies have access to all of the resources they need to succeed, such as study materials, support forums, and mentorship programs. By providing ongoing support and resources, Skill-Lync is able to create a positive learning environment that encourages students and companies to continue using the platform.

This includes a Learning Management System (LMS), challenges, one-on-one calls, study plans, projects, career services, and interviews, all provided until the student is placed.

Phase 1: Understanding the product

I was assigned to post-sales but I started working on small projects and learned about the platform. I then moved on to working with the pre-sales team, learning Skill-Lync's courses and services. Through this experience, I gained a better understanding of the needs and interests of potential clients, which helped me later when working on the revamp of the entire product experience.

We began by setting up meetings with different teams, including TSE, CX, Engineering, Marketing, and Students, both online and offline.

Initiating calls and scheduling meetings proved to be the most difficult part and took a lot of time.

Unfortunately, there was no documentation available, and each team had its own understanding of the product, including the team leaders. With things changing every day, it was necessary to connect with everyone, but most of the time, people were not available.

After some research, we found that the collaborative environment was not good enough, and we failed to make progress. So, I approached the team leader to set up a weekly call to get everyone on the same page. We held a weekly call to align everyone, and the call was recorded and a release email was sent to everyone.

Our research began with students, but to reach them, I connected with the CX team since they were in contact with students every day. Each student had a dedicated mentor who could assist with anything from loans and guidance to motivation, doubts, and learning materials. If necessary, this could initiate a TSE call.

Phase 2: Ideation

Analysis of the main issues within the product and prioritizing the issues to be fixed.

The company’s main focus was to improve the student experience and UI refresh of LMS.

I worked on the following:

  1. Revamping Student Assessment Test (SAT)

1. One on One session

A one-on-one session with a student is a tutoring session between a TSE (Technical support Assistant who have an understanding of engineering course material) and an individual student. These sessions are typically designed to provide personalized attention, support, and guidance to the student in a focused and individualized setting.

One-on-one sessions are conducted online through google meet, and they can be used to address a variety of learning needs, including academic tutoring, test preparation, and study skills development.

Issues found after interacting with students and TSE

After conducting several meetings with students and interacting with more than 30 employees, I have identified the main issues that need to be corrected.

  1. Notification for scheduled O3 is not uniform

Sometimes it is in google meet, sometimes it is in zoom. Siddarth (DADS TSE) doesn't get a notification for zoom meetings in the mail and he uses the flock bot. For TD in google meet, I can join with google calendar, but for the one in Zoom, it is not easy. (Sudharsana, TL DADS) Can we have a standard notification where we get all of them in Gmail or all of them shown in google calendar?

  1. No option for transferring the O3s

The TSE does not have the option to transfer the O3 to another TSE in case of unavailability or emergency.

  1. Feedback and Ratings

There are no ratings available by the student for the TSE after the session to gauge his/her feedback

  1. Several O3s lapping

Most of them have multiple sessions assigned at the same time. Some students request a 15-minute session and some request 30 minute or a 1-hour session. Is there a way where when somebody selects a 3 to 3:15 slot in 15 minutes, that 3 to 3-15 is made unavailable in the one-hour time slot?

  1. Not evenly distributed

Sessions are not evenly distributed to the TSEs on the roster in a group

  1. The status of the O3 is not updated on the portal

Status remains unattended when TSE has actually attended it. DADS and Mechanical TSEs have been facing this issue.

  1. Need a buffer for 5 minutes between O3

For brushing up or restroom break. Sometimes sessions happen continuously.

  1. Uniformity

To have a uniform standard. The student was given both Zoom and google meet links.

  1. Students are not describing their issues in the description of the O3

We request you add a drop-down menu where students can pick up which week it is and which sub-topic it is. Our TSE can give you topic-wise details. We had 5 instances this week, where students joined for completely different circumstances. A TSE had to handle 3 customer support related to queries in O3. The student arranged an O3 to inform us that she doesn't have access to the next course. Similarly, students attending the live batch programs are booking O3 and asking for help to solve challenges or doubts.  Another student books an O3 to solve external questions (as in interview-related or assignments in school/college). We also have a scenario where a student just writes basic doubts, books a 15-minute session, and then ask advanced topics. By giving a drop-down menu, we can easily filter out students who should not be getting O3 for their personal interview questions or live sessions, or customer support-related queries.

  1. A slot marked unavailable can not be marked available in the portal

A slot marked unavailable in the portal can not be marked available. Sometimes TSE estimates a particular time and blocks their calendar, once their job is done, they would like to get back and work. But they are not able to unmark unavailable. Kindly help us out here.

  1. Did not attend Status-Student or agent

Please add 1 more piece of data to the status of the O3 as to who did not attend it - the Agent or the student (at present we have to open the O3 session to view the details)

  1. TL does not get Notification of pending requests from TSE in the portal dashboard: (TSE rostering requests)

Without the notification, TL would not know who is marking their unavailability in the portal. If not approved, the slots are booked by students. It is more convenient if the TL gets a message.

  1. The available days for assigning O3 meetings were not more than 7 days

The entire calendar is displayed in the user interface, allowing students to navigate to the previous day or the next seven days to book appointments. Students can book appointments for previous days as well.

Insights from Data Analysis

O3 Booking Pattern

  • ~70% of all the sessions in May have been booked by the Customer
  • For ~43% of the active students in May only 1 O3 got scheduled in May
  • Total reported incident of desired slot not available(20 May to 31 May ) is 6757 (97.64% )
  • The Total “No slot available” incident is 2576 (~37%) for 1710 unique students
  • ~16% of active students have booked more than 6 O3 slots in May

O3 Scheduled vs Completion

  • ~ 48% of the sessions were booked and scheduled for the same day
  • ~50% sessions & ~35% sessions were of 15 min & 30 min respectively
  • ~29% of sessions were “No Show” (~63% cases “Student No Show, ~18% cases “Agent No Show” )
  • For ~15% of Students who booked the O3 Slot by themselves, the session did not get completed, either it got canceled or it was not attended
  • ~50% of the students scheduled more than 3 sessions by themselves (in May) , while only ~37% of all such multiple sessions were completed
  • For ~28% of the Active students, 4 or more O3s were booked, out of which ~9% of the students did not attend 4 or more sessions
  • As the difference between the booking date and the session schedule date increases, the chances of Student “No Shows” increase (by 5% for each day)

O3 Slot Utilization

  • 30 min Scheduled O3 gets over earlier by ~12 to ~15 min in 32.19% sessions
  • 45 min Scheduled O3 gets over earlier by ~12 min to ~15 min in 36.12% sessions
  • The average utilization of O3 based on the scheduled duration and Actual duration of sessions is approx. ~53.34%
  • Average TSE utilization is ~ 31%

O3 Effectiveness

  • The number of Challenge submissions has increased in May 2022 by ~23% as compared to April 2022

O3 Booking backend Logic

  • The O3 Logic takes into account the count of slots already booked and not the duration of the slots booked.
    • TSE having less number of slots invariable of the duration of the booked slots will be assigned a new slot( if the slot is available)
    • The Courses which are not part of the rostering are also getting displayed in the O3 Scheduling dashboard as course display is based on the Student’s Enrolment

Solution Proposed

Did Not Attend

29% of booked O3 sessions were “No Show” (~63% cases “Student No Show, ~18% cases “Agent No Show” )

Proposed Solution

  • Student Not Showing Up
    • Whatsapp Reminder 24 hours/12 hour/6 hour/1 hour/15 min before the Scheduled O3, with the option given to the student to confirm the availability to attend the session (Constraint- additional cost: Alternative way is sending email reminders or notification on the LMS)
    • In case the student “Do Not attend” 2 consecutive sessions, a trigger will be created to notify the CX team for HAPC
  • Agent Not Showing up
    • Real-time notification to the Team Lead if the Agent does not attend within 5 minutes of scheduled timings, the team lead will have the option to reassign the session to another TSE who is available
    • In case of more than 3 “No Shows” within a week or 5 “No Shows” within a month the Agent will be excluded from the group incentive for the month/week

Multiple Booking & Cancellation

  • ~28% of the Students booked more than 4 O3 in May, out of which ~9% of students did not attend 4 or more O3
  • ~16% of students have booked more than 6 O3 slots in May

Proposed Solution

Mean Session Duration of O3 in a month(Completed) = 21.28 min

95% Confidence Interval = 21 min  to 21.64 min

  • Restriction on booking more than 60 min session (liberal estimate) at any point by the Students
  • The cumulative difference between the duration of the total session booked by the student and the scheduled duration for the session attended by the student should not be more than 60 min for the student to be able to book a new session by themselves
  • In the Scenario where the student is not able to schedule a session due to kick in of such restriction, they can “Request CX Agent” to book a session (also, CX Agent can override and reset the counter in case they feel the student had genuine reasons for not attending the session.

Non-Utilization of Scheduled Sessions

30 min session and 45 min session gets over earlier by 15 min in approx 32.19% and 36.12% of cases

Proposed Solution

Correlating the Session type with the session duration and putting a “soft restriction” on changing the session duration

The slot duration needs to be correlated with the “Type of session” (to be introduced)  selected by the student.
