Improving Student Experience with Ed-tech Platform

Revamping Skill-Lync's post-sales student experience — from NPS analysis and qualitative research to redesigning mentoring sessions and the LMS interface.

About

📕 Project Overview

Skill-Lync is an ed-tech platform that provides industry-relevant technical courses in Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, Electronics, Medical Technology & Computer Science Engineering. The company provides job-assistance programs and corporate upskilling (B2B).

I was with the product team (4-5 PMs, 2 designers). The team was split into pre-sales and post-sales teams. Our major project was to revamp the entire product experience.

Pre-sales

Responsible for reaching out to potential students and companies to promote courses, demo calls, TSE discussions, payment, loan processing, and onboarding until LMS entry.

Post-sales

Ensuring positive learning experience — LMS, challenges, 1-on-1 calls, study plans, projects, career services, and interviews until placement.

Approach

🎨 Design Process

Phase 1

🔍 Understanding the Product

I was assigned to post-sales but worked across pre-sales to understand user needs. We began setting up meetings with TSE, CX, Engineering, Marketing, and Students.

Initiating calls and scheduling meetings proved to be the most difficult part. Unfortunately, there was no documentation available, and each team had its own understanding of the product. I set up a weekly alignment call with recorded notes and release emails.

Qualitative

📞 User Interviews & Reach-out Summary

Background: Program completion rate (completing 3 mandatory courses) was only ~20%. We interviewed learners across various dropout stages to understand blockers.

Reached out to 152 unique students (Passives & Detractors): 49% had no specific issue (54 agreed to change rating to Promoter), 27% raised content concerns, 4.27% reported O3 mentoring slot unavailability, and 3.66% were dissatisfied with technical support resolution.

Phase 2

💡 Ideation & Module Focus

Focused on two key areas: 1. One-on-One Tutoring Sessions (O3) and 2. Revamping Student Assessment Tests (SAT).

O3 Redesign

📱 13 Operational Issues in Mentoring (O3)

1. Non-uniform links
Mix of Zoom and Google Meet links caused confusion.
2. No transfer option
TSEs could not reassign O3 sessions during emergencies.
3. Overlapping slots
15/30/60 min slots conflicted, causing double-booking.
4. No buffer breaks
No 5-minute break between continuous tutoring calls.
5. Unclear descriptions
Students booked O3 for non-technical CX or loan issues.
6. Slot unavailability
97.64% reported desired slots unavailable; 37% couldn't find any slot.

Solutions

✅ Solutions Implemented

UI & Scheduling Changes (P0)

  1. Replaced description field with a mandatory Session Type Dropdown (Challenge Support, Project, Concept Help, Software doubt).
  2. Added Capture Last Session Feedback requirement before new bookings.
  3. Introduced "Request Callback" feature notifying CX when no slots are available.
  4. Created a "Next Week Request Queue" pre-booking option.

Attendance & Booking Restrictions

Student No-Show Remedy

WhatsApp reminders at 24h, 12h, 6h, 1h, 15min. If a student misses 2 consecutive calls → CX HAPC trigger.

Agent No-Show Remedy

Real-time alert to Team Lead if agent doesn't join within 5 minutes → TL can reassign to an available TSE immediately.

Executive Pitch Deck

Skill-Lync Student Experience Redesign

  • Objective: Increase 3-course program completion rate beyond 20% baseline
  • Scope: NPS analysis across 2,141 surveys, O3 mentoring revamp, LMS interface redesign
  • Impact: Reduced O3 no-shows from 28.26% and improved technical support utilization
🚀 Product Focus: Streamlined post-sales engagement to drive student course completion and job placement.
Data Discovery

NPS Analysis (2,141 Surveys)

  • 66.88% Overall NPS: Analyzed feedback split across CAE, CFD, Civil, CSE, Data Science, and Electrical domains.
  • Detractor Root Cause: 91% of CSE domain detractors cited course content explanation & video clarity.
📊 Key Insight: Support slot unavailability directly led to lower NPS scores among active students.
Mentoring Revamp

O3 Mentoring Session Optimization

  • 28.26% Baseline No-Show Rate: Identified non-uniform booking links and lack of reminder triggers.
  • Structured Dropdown: Replaced open text field with specific session categories (Doubt, Project, Concept).
✅ Solution: Automated WhatsApp reminders and real-time TL reassignment for missed slots.
UI / UX Output

Redesigned LMS & Mentoring Interface

  • Modern Design System: Clean visual typography and structured navigation for course modules.
  • Seamless Booking: Integrated 1-click mentoring appointment queue.
🎨 Outcome: Highly intuitive learning dashboard driving higher daily active engagement.
Executive Summary

Optimized Learning Journey for Higher Course Completions

  • Delivered data-backed recommendations across 10+ technical engineering domains.
  • Restructured the 1-on-1 tutoring ecosystem to maximize mentor availability.
🌟 Product Impact: Transformed post-sales support into an active driver of student success.
