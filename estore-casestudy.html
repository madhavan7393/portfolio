Overview

📒 Introduction

QueueBuster’s Khata was a new feature which was added to QueueuBuster android application.

Problem

🤔 Why Khata?

QueueBuster has customers from Retail Business, Restaurant Business, Grocery Stores, Spa and Salon, and more. QueueBuster’s major focus is on the unorganized retail industry like General store or Kirana store. This khata feature can be helpful for these merchants.

Context

📖 What is Khata?

There are Kirana stores in every corner of India, where loyal customers buy the products on credit. Shopkeepers maintain all the customer's credit in a notebook, this is where Khata can help.

For example : Sunil buys groceries everyday from Ratna Store. He pays for all the groceries bought in end of the week. Shopkeeper notes all the purchases Sunil made throughout the week in a notebook and recieves payment end of the week.

Khata can record all the transactions easily made with the customer which makes this process much simpler for the merchant. The merchant can also ask the customer to pay through SMS, Whatsapp, email, etc. anytime with a payment link.

Design

🔄 User flow for Khata

We looked for products which were offering similar features. After researching we started to analyze how the merchants would be using it if this feature was built.

We found that there were two main transactions that will be performed by the users(merchant) :

  1. Give Credit
  2. Accept payment

We made this generic user flow for both the transactions.

Quick Summary

Khata — Digital Credit Ledger for Kirana Stores

  • Target Audience: Kirana store owners and unorganized retail merchants in India.
  • Core Problem: Paper notebook credit tracking leading to lost records and collection delays.
  • Solution: Embedded digital Khata feature with instant payment link sharing via WhatsApp.
🚀 Strategic Impact: Drive daily merchant retention within the QueueBuster POS ecosystem.
User Flow

Dual Transaction Model: Give Credit & Accept Payment

  • Give Credit: Log purchase items with receipt photo proof in 2 steps.
  • Accept Payment: Record cash payment or generate dynamic UPI/SMS payment link.
💡 Usability Focus: Designed for rapid operation during busy store hours.
Key Features

Image Proof & Payment Link Automation

  • Bill Image Upload: Attach photo of physical receipt or goods for transaction dispute resolution.
  • Lock Paid Transactions: Auto-lock completed online payments to prevent accidental editing.
✅ Trust Factor: Building transparency between shopkeeper and regular buyers.
Quick Summary

Digitizing Kirana Credit Relationships

  • Transformed manual notebook record-keeping into a seamless POS feature.
  • Reduced payment collection cycles for small retail merchants across India.
🌟 Ecosystem Expansion: Added sticky value-add service to QueueBuster Android POS.
