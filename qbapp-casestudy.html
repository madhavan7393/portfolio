Case Study

Improving QueueBuster Android Application

Redesigning the POS Android app to reduce support calls — tackling feature visibility, cart customization, product information, and complex create-product flows.

Company
QueueBuster
Year
2021
Role
Product Designer
Platform
Android

Overview

🎯 Approach

Our customers were facing a hard time while operating the point of sale application. Our customer support team had to explain everything in detail about the important features but we wanted to minimize the support work so that customers can learn to use the app by themselves. We wanted to redesign some important features so that users can understand them easily.

We collected all the possible complaints from the support team in order to solve our problem.

Research

🔍 Findings of our research

Pain points of our customer

  1. Lack of visibility of some features in QueueBuster app.
  2. Customers were not able to edit or customize products in the cart in Food/Electronic industry.
  3. No detailed information of the product in the cart to differentiate two same products.
  4. The customer felt create product process very complex.

Reason for the above pain points

  1. We were showing all the features inside the hamburger menu. Our landing page was the billing section. Hard time finding all the features.
  2. Users had a hard time customizing the food product on the cart screen. There were lots of scenarios when the user perform any operation and sometimes the product was complex especially in case of a combo food products.
  3. We were not displaying any information about product like IMEI number, serial number of products.
  4. Create product form had lots of input fields but was not segregated properly for easy understanding.

Strategy

🎯 Goals

With all the findings we decided to :

  • To make a home screen that would comprise all the important features of the application.
  • Open Product should be given more preference and we renamed it to “Quick Bill”.
  • Redesigning the cart page.
  • Proper segregation of input fields was needed and mandatory input fields were decided in create product form.

Quick Summary

QueueBuster Android POS UX Optimization

  • Goal: Reduce customer support calls by making the POS application self-explanatory.
  • Key Interventions: New dedicated Home Screen, redesigned Cart customization, Quick Bill flow.
  • Approach: User-centered redesign driven by support ticket analytics.
🚀 Outcome: Drastic reduction in customer onboarding friction and support ticket volume.
Research

Cashier Workflow Bottlenecks

  • Hidden Features: Hamburger menu hid essential store management options.
  • Cart Customization: Combo food items and electronics variant selection caused delays during billing.
💡 Insight: Cashiers need high-frequency actions immediately visible on launch.
UI Redesign

Surfacing Key Modules on Home Screen

  • Dedicated Home: Prominently displays Catalog, Billing, Inventory, and Quick Bill.
  • Quick Bill: 1-tap open billing flow for fast-moving retail transactions.
✅ Usability: Eliminated menu hunting for cashiers.
Cart UX

Streamlined Cart & Product Creation

  • Product Identifiers: Added clear serial/IMEI tracking in cart.
  • Form Segregation: Organized complex input fields into logical sections.
🎨 Experience: Intuitive, multi-industry checkout support.
Quick Summary

User-Centered Android POS Experience

  • Replaced customer support dependence with intuitive self-serve design.
  • Supported retail, restaurant, and grocery verticals on a single Android app.
🌟 Result: Scalable UI foundation for QueueBuster's Android hardware fleet.
